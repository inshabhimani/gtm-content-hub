# Nexora Consulting – GTM Content Hub

## 1. Company Background

Nexora Consulting is a fictional consulting company that provides business, technology and digital transformation services to clients.

The company publishes marketing content such as:
- Industry webpages
- Service pages
- Case studies
- Blog articles
- Landing pages

As the company grows, its content library becomes difficult to manage.

## 2. Business Problem

The company has hundreds of existing webpages and documents. However, many have quality and organisation issues:

- Inconsistent formatting and HTML structure.
- Missing page titles and descriptions.
- Images without alternative text (alt text).
- Broken hyperlinks.
- Outdated content.
- Duplicate or incomplete metadata.
- Difficulty finding and reusing existing content.

These issues increase manual work and make content management inefficient.

## 3. Proposed Solution

Build a centralised **GTM Content Hub** to organise, clean, standardise and monitor marketing content.

The platform will include:

1. Content Library – Store and organise webpages.
2. Content Templates – Standardise industry, service and case-study pages.
3. Python Migration Tool – Automatically clean legacy HTML pages.
4. Quality Assurance – Identify missing metadata, broken links and accessibility issues.
5. AI Assistance – Suggest alternative text and improved meta descriptions, subject to human review.
6. Analytics Dashboard – Monitor content quality and migration progress.
7. Issue Tracking – Record problems and track their resolution.

## 4. Target Users

- Marketing Team – Create and manage content.
- Content Managers – Review and approve webpages.
- SEO Team – Monitor metadata and content quality.
- Management – Monitor migration progress and performance.

## 5. Project Objectives

- Organise legacy marketing content.
- Standardise webpage structure.
- Reduce repetitive manual cleaning.
- Identify content quality issues.
- Improve content accessibility.
- Track migration progress through measurable KPIs.

## 6. Success Metrics (KPIs)

| KPI | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Migration Completion Rate | Percentage of pages successfully migrated. |
| Metadata Completeness | Percentage of pages with required metadata. |
| Broken Link Rate | Percentage of checked links that are broken. |
| Alt Text Coverage | Percentage of images with alt text. |
| QA Pass Rate | Percentage of pages passing defined quality checks. |
| Issue Resolution Rate | Percentage of identified issues resolved. |

## 7. Project Scope

### Included
- 50 synthetic legacy webpages.
- Three standardised HTML templates.
- Python-based migration and cleaning.
- Automated quality checks.
- Mock issue tracking.
- AI-assisted content suggestions.
- Excel or Power BI dashboard.

### Not Included in Phase 1
- Real company data.
- Production CMS integration.
- Live user authentication.
- Automated publishing to a real website.
- Predictive AI features.

## 8. Expected Outcome

A working prototype demonstrating how a consulting company can migrate, standardise, monitor and improve its marketing content using Python, web technologies, analytics and AI assistance.
